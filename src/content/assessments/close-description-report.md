## The brief

> Describe one experience so precisely that a stranger could recognise it in
> themselves.

Choose one experience from unit 1 (cold shock, uncontrollable laughter, the
earned ache) or unit 2 (unstructured boredom, the drift into sleep, the spark
of intuition) that you actually underwent during that week's practicum. This
is not a summary of the practicum brief — it is your own account, written with
the discipline descriptive phenomenology demands: bracket your assumptions
about what the experience "means," and describe only what was actually
present to you, in the order it arrived.

The room this brief leaves is in *which* experience you pick and how deep you
go. The judgement it tests is whether you can hold description and
interpretation apart on the page, not just in principle.

## What you submit

A single written report, 1200–1800 words, as a page in this repo or a linked
document. Cite your own practicum log as primary material — quote your
in-the-moment notes directly rather than paraphrasing them from memory
months later.
