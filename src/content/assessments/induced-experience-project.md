## The brief

Pick one target experience from weeks 1–9 and design a deliberate attempt to
induce it — in yourself, or in a participant who has given informed consent
and knows they can stop at any point. This is the semester's midpoint
practicum, scaled up: instead of following a given brief, you write one.

A good design states, in advance, what conditions you expect to produce the
experience and why. The project is not judged on whether the induction
"worked" in the sense of definitely producing the target experience — a
careful account of a failed induction, with a plausible diagnosis of why, is
a legitimate and gradeable outcome.

## What you submit

A written design-and-outcome document, plus any supporting material (a log,
audio notes, photographs of a physical setup) that helps substantiate what
happened. Close with a short reflection: what your outcome does or doesn't
tell you about whether a well-designed induction could ever guarantee an
inner experience, not just its usual outward signs.
