## The brief

Across the semester you've induced, noticed and described twelve different
experiences, and sat through twelve lectures pairing each one with a concept
from philosophy of mind or cognitive science. The final portfolio asks you to
stop collecting evidence and actually argue something with it: **can a good
enough description ever close the gap with having the experience, or is the
gap categorical?**

Pick a position — closes it, narrows it but never closes it, or the gap is
unreal and the whole premise is confused — and defend it using your own
semester as evidence. Then take your strongest objection seriously and answer
it, rather than only citing practicums that agree with you.

## What you submit

A single portfolio document, 2500–3500 words, that can stand alone without
the reader having followed the course week by week. Quote your own practicum
log and induction project directly where they support your argument.
