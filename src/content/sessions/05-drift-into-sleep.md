The hardest experience in this course to study directly, because the tool
doing the studying is the thing that switches off. This week isn't about
sleep itself — it's about the last stretch of road before it, the part
usually lost entirely because nothing is there afterwards to remember it.

*This week's $100 experience grant lands today. See
[policies](/policies/) for how to spend it, defer it to a later week, or
account for it with a receipt.*

## Before the practicum

No screens in the final thirty minutes before you try this — screens keep you
too alert to catch the drift, and the point is to fall asleep close to
normally while still holding a thread of attention on the process.

## In the practicum

As you lie down, try to keep a light, non-interfering watch on your own
thoughts — the intention is closer to journaling in real time than to
meditating. Notice when thoughts stop following each other logically and
start bending into something dreamlike. Most nights this thread breaks before
you expect it to; that break is the data.

## Afterwards

Write down the last thing you remember thinking clearly, and the first thing
that didn't make sense, as close together as you can place them. If you
genuinely can't locate a boundary — if it feels like there wasn't one — say
that instead of inventing one; a seamless transition is itself a finding.
