Week 1 asked you to notice a sensation arrive from outside you. This week asks
you to notice a state that arrives from *between* people, and that mostly
resists being summoned alone. Uncontrollable laughter is one of the few
everyday experiences with an unambiguous physical tell — you can point to
exactly when it stopped being a decision.

*No grant lands this week — funding runs on alternate weeks. If you're
drawing on money carried over from an earlier week to fund this practicum,
see [policies](/policies/) for the receipt requirement.*

## Before the practicum

Bring something that has reliably broken you before: a person, a video, an
in-joke, whatever your history says is not a safe bet against your composure.
Don't bring something merely funny; bring your known weak point.

## In the practicum

Work in pairs or small groups. Let the group's laughter do what it does —
notice how quickly it becomes self-sustaining, independent of whatever
triggered it. The point to catch is the handoff: the instant the laugh stops
answering to the joke and starts answering to itself.

## Afterwards

Try, alone and later, to get back to that state by recalling the same trigger.
For almost everyone this fails, or produces something noticeably smaller. Say
in your note what was missing the second time — the audience, the
unpredictability, something else. A state you can't reliably re-enter by
description is exactly the kind this course exists to sit with.
