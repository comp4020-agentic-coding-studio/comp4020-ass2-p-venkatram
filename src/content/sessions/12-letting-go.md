The last practicum, and the one built to look backward as much as forward.
Letting go is the release the rest of the semester has been circling: week 1
was the body seized by something outside its control; this week is choosing,
consciously, to stop seizing back.

*No grant lands this week. This is also the last week to account for every
dollar disbursed across the semester — see [policies](/policies/) if
anything remains unspent or unreceipted.*

## Before the practicum

Name something specific you are currently trying to control — a
conversation's outcome, a piece of work's reception, another person's
decision, your own future. Identify the exact behaviour that *is* the
controlling: the checking, the rehearsing, the message you keep re-drafting.

## In the practicum

Stop that specific behaviour, deliberately, for the length of the practicum.
Not distraction from it — a conscious decision to release it, held for as
long as you can sustain it. Notice what happens physically when you do:
tension leaving somewhere, a held breath finally going out, or nothing at all.

## Afterwards

Write the note, then write a short closing paragraph linking this week back to
one earlier week of your choosing — cold shock, the idling mind, awe, grief,
whichever one it genuinely connects to for you. The semester does not resolve
into one tidy account of consciousness, and this practicum asks you to let go
of expecting it to.
