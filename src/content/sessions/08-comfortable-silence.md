Every experience so far in this course has been something you could, in
principle, have alone. This one can't be manufactured solo — it only exists
between two people, which makes it the course's first real test of whether
introspection can say anything true about an experience that isn't entirely
yours.

*No grant lands this week — funding runs on alternate weeks. If you're
drawing on money carried over from an earlier week to fund this practicum,
see [policies](/policies/) for the receipt requirement.*

## Before the practicum

Choose someone you already trust enough that silence with them wouldn't
normally feel like a failure of conversation. Agree explicitly, before you
start, not to fill gaps — this has to be a deliberate silence, not an
accidental one that happens to work out.

## In the practicum

Sit together with no task and no phones for the full twenty minutes. Resist
the urge to check whether it's "working." If it turns awkward, that's data
too — write down where the shift happened rather than abandoning the attempt.

## Afterwards

Compare notes with the other person honestly, including any place your
accounts don't match. A comfortable silence that only one of you experienced
as comfortable is a different, and equally real, finding — and a useful one
for the report due at the end of this unit.
