Wren Okafor trained in cognitive science before turning to phenomenology out
of frustration: papers on emotion, memory and perception kept describing
experiences from the outside, in terms a subject would barely recognise as
their own. The practicum method taught in this course grew out of a stubborn
question from that period — if a description is any good, shouldn't reading
it get you at least a little closer to the thing itself?

Wren teaches the weekly lecture, pairing each week's induced experience with
the concept from philosophy of mind or cognitive science that frames it —
without pretending the frame closes the gap it's trying to describe.
