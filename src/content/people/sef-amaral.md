Sef Amaral comes to this course from a background in contemplative and
somatic practice, not from a philosophy department — which turns out to
matter, because inducing an experience on purpose and then noticing it
without immediately theorising it are learnable skills, not given ones. Sef
runs the weekly practicum sessions: setting up each week's induction safely,
keeping the noticing period free of premature interpretation, and holding the
room for whichever weeks turn out to need more care than others.

Sef is also the first point of contact for anything raised by the [policies
page](/policies/), particularly around the grief practicum in week 11.
