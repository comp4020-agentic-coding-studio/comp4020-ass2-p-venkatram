Grief research has largely moved away from the popular five-stages model
towards accounts that emphasise an ongoing, non-linear relationship with an
absence — "continuing bonds" theory, for instance, describes grief not as a
process with an endpoint but as a lasting, shifting relationship with what's
gone. This lecture stays theoretical for a reason: this is the one experience
in the course this week's practicum does not ask you to induce.

## Outline

- why the stage model of grief is now considered too tidy by most researchers
