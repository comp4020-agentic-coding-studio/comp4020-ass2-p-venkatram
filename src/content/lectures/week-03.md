Two-factor theories of emotion argue that a bodily state (raised heart rate,
muscular fatigue, adrenaline) is ambiguous on its own — the same signal reads
as anxiety or excitement depending on how it's interpreted in context. The
earned ache extends that idea to satisfaction: the same muscular fatigue
reads as depletion after an aimless workout and as reward after labor with a
result. The sensation doesn't change; the meaning wrapped around it does.
