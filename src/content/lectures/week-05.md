Every experience in this course has one instrument available to study it:
whatever is doing the noticing. Falling asleep is the case where that
instrument switches off partway through, which is exactly why the
hypnagogic state — the drifting, logic-loosened stretch right before sleep —
has been so hard to pin down scientifically, and so persistently interesting
to artists and researchers who study it anyway.

## Outline

- hypnagogia: what's actually been measured (EEG changes, hallucinatory
  imagery, loosened association) versus what's introspectively reported
- why the last thing you remember is not the same as the last thing that
  happened
- what it means that the most reliable data point most people have is "I
  don't remember losing the thread" rather than a described boundary
