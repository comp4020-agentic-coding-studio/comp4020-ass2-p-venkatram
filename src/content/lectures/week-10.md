Research on awe describes it as arising from an encounter with something that
overwhelms your existing mental categories — vastness, in scale or in
significance, that your ordinary frameworks weren't built to hold. One
consistently reported effect is a temporary shrinking of the sense of self:
people report feeling smaller, less central, and — counterintuitively — more
connected rather than more isolated, in the same moment.

## Outline

- awe as a response to "vastness" that exceeds your current mental
  categories, and why that framing fits a night sky especially well
- the "small self" effect: why feeling smaller so often reports as more
  alive rather than more diminished
- opening unit 4: the self measured against something enormously larger than
  itself, three different ways
