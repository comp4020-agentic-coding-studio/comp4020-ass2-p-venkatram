The somatic marker hypothesis proposes that bodily states — a tightened chest,
a held breath — aren't just downstream side effects of emotion but part of
how the mind evaluates a situation in the first place. Relief is a clean
place to test the idea informally: many people report the bodily
release — the exhale, the dropped shoulders — arriving a beat before the
verbal thought "I don't need to worry about that anymore," rather than after
it.
