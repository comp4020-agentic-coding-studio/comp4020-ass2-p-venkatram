# Experience Subjective Consciousness

SLOP1163 — Week 1

## A different kind of course

Every course on the mind you've taken so far hands you a description and
calls it understanding.

This one doesn't.

---

{/* _class: quote */}

> "Consciousness is what makes the mind-body problem really intractable."
>
> — Thomas Nagel

---

## The hard problem, stated plainly

Not: *how does the brain produce behaviour.*

That's hard, but it's ordinary science.

The hard problem is: **why is there something it is like** to be a brain
doing that, at all?

---

## Nagel's bat

You could know every physical fact about echolocation.

Neuron firing rates. Auditory cortex maps. Wing muscle timing.

You still wouldn't know what it's like to *be* the bat.

---

{/* _class: centered */}

## The explanatory gap

physical story → ??? → felt experience

The "???" is the whole course.

---

## Why this course is a practicum

You cannot read your way across the explanatory gap.

So we don't try to.

Each week: **induce → notice → describe**, in that order.

---

## The semester, in four units

1. **Immediate sensation** — the body registers something all at once
2. **The idling mind** — consciousness with nothing pinning it down
3. **Memory & others** — experience across time, or between people
4. **Scale & release** — the self against something larger than itself

---

## Twelve inductions

Cold water. Laughter. Earned exhaustion. Boredom. Sleep's edge. Intuition.

Scent-memory. Shared silence. Relief. Awe. Grief. Letting go.

One per week. Yours to actually do.
